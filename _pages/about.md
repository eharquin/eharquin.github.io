:wave: Hi, I'm Enzo Harquin, a PhD student at the [Gaspard-Monge Computer Science Laboratory (LIGM)](https://ligm.univ-eiffel.fr/home/), part of [Gustave Eiffel University](https://www.univ-gustave-eiffel.fr/en/) in France.

My doctoral research focuses on **[Geometric Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_algebra)** (GA), a mathematical framework that provides an elegant bridge between abstract mathematics and geometric intuition. I explore how problems in computer vision and computer graphics can be generalized or solved more elegantly using geometric algebra methods.

My recent work has centered on porting computer vision and computer graphics techniques into the geometric algebra framework, with particular emphasis on Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA).

I hold an Engineering Degree in Computer Science from [UTC](https://www.utc.fr/en/) and a Master's in Image Science from [IGM](https://igm.univ-gustave-eiffel.fr/formations/master-2-sciences-de-limage/) at Gustave Eiffel University, where I graduated with honors.
